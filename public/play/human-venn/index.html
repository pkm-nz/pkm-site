The Human Venn Diagram

A Dada performance for about 80 people: seven stretchy bands, six silent questions and ten minutes of dance music on the grass at 10pm.

0:00 / 1:32

How it worked

  1. Everyone gathers on the lawn and one band goes around the whole crowd.
  2. Paul silently flips a question card. Everyone who answers yes gets a new coloured band around them, held by Brenda's helpers.
  3. People shuffle so each band holds only its yes group, and the bands overlap like a Venn diagram.
  4. Six questions, each more confronting than the last.
  5. Count the bands you're inside. The most bands wins.

On the night the soundtrack was "Sensoria" by Cabaret Voltaire. That track can't be included here, so the animation plays a simple dance beat instead (turn your phone's silent switch off to hear it). You can also play "Sensoria" alongside and mute the beat. The crowd ended up so squashed together that everyone started dancing, which added the chaos.

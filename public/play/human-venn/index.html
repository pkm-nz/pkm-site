How it worked

  1. Everyone gathers on the lawn and one band goes around the whole crowd.
  2. Paul silently flips a question card. Everyone who answers yes gets a new coloured band around them, held by Brenda.
  3. People shuffle so each band holds only its yes group, and the bands overlap like a Venn diagram.
  4. Six questions, each more confronting than the last.
  5. Count the bands you're inside. The most bands wins.

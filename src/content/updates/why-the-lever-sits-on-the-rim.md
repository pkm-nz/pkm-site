project: sand-sphere

excerpt: A half turn mirrors the shell through the beach, so a handle anywhere up the dome would end up buried. The rim is the only place that finishes at the surface.

The shell turns half a revolution around a rod that lies flat on the sand. Any point above the beach ends up the same distance below it.

So the lever pins to a flat tab at the rim. Halfway through, standing upright, it gets re-pinned to the other face, and that pause becomes part of the performance.

excerpt: The Chimp was completed back in 2012. Even though I tested the concept with a smaller sculpture prior to the Chimp, I wasn't really sure if the 2d/3d thing would work with this type of acrylic and at this scale. Holding up a few 10cm square acrylic samples to the light was all I had to go on. It was only after everything had been laser cut and assembled, that I could stand back and really gauge if it was successful. Things couldn't have gone better, but it was quite a gamble. Fast forward 14 years and now I can create a perfect simulation of the sculpture to validate the concept prior to cutting anything. Good fun for sure, but not quite as thrilling as that 'It works! It works!' feeling.

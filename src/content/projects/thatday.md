Most of us have no clear picture of how much we'll need to retire, so we put off thinking about it. ThatDay is built to fix that. You answer a few questions, for yourself or as a couple, and it shows roughly what you'll need and what you're on track for.

From there ThatDay helps you find practical steps: places to free up savings, smarter ways to spend, and plain-language articles on retirement and money. It's a planning tool, not financial advice, and it's run as a charity so it's free for everyone.

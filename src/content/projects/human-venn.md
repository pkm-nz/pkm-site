intro: For many years a good friend of mine has run a themed event where people present a painting, sculpture, performance, poem or song, depending on that year’s theme. In 2015 it was “Who’s your Dada?”, a celebration of the movement we know and love. I love the mix of chaos, chance and absurdity in Dada, so I figured something involving audience participation was worth a go. We went on last, when the crowd of about 80 was nicely loose. I was spotlit and never said a word; the instructions were written on large flip cards for everyone to follow. With each question, Brenda stretched another coloured band around everyone who answered yes, until the crowd became a living Venn diagram. Eighty fairly tipsy people squashed together inside elastic bands, dancing to “Sensoria” by Cabaret Voltaire (see what I did there?) What’s not to like? And we won!

The brief was a celebration of Dada, and every entry had to embody its spirit. I wanted something that used the guests themselves as the material: order imposed by rules, then chaos as the crowd got squashed together and started dancing.
